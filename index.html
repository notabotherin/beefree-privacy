Privacy Policy — BeeFree

Effective date: 18 September 2026 · App: BeeFree (life.beefree.app)

Contact: notabotherin@gmail.com

What the app stores

BeeFree is a single-player garden sim. Progress (hive, pollen, cosmetics, settings) is saved on the device using Android app preferences / local storage.

v1 does not require an account and does not upload your garden to a studio server.

Purchases

If you buy cosmetics or Amber on Google Play, Google processes the payment. We receive a purchase token and product id so we can unlock the item you paid for. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Use Restore Purchases on the LOOK tab if you reinstall.

Optional analytics (off until enabled)

If Crashlytics or Analytics are added in a later build, this policy will be updated and the Play Data safety form will match. Until then, the app does not include those SDKs.

Ads

v1 does not show ads in front of flight or deposit. If rewarded ads are enabled later, they will be opt-in (extra daily or a short scent highlight), never a yield multiplier. Remove Ads is a cosmetic convenience SKU.

Children

The intended content rating is Everyone / 7+ (no threat animals in v1). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app deletes the on-device save. Purchases remain on your Google account and can be restored.

Changes

We will update this file and the Play listing if data practices change.
